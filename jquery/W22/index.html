🏛️ About the Institute

History & Vision

PICT was founded in 1983 by Pune Vidyarthi Griha. It is one of Pune's most prestigious engineering institutes, known for producing industry-ready engineers. Approved by AICTE and accredited by NBA.

Mission

To impart quality technical education, develop innovative thinking, and foster professional integrity so graduates contribute meaningfully to society and the nation.

Accreditation

✅ NAAC — A+ Grade  |  ✅ NBA Accredited
✅ AICTE Approved  |  ✅ SPPU Autonomous

📋 Admission Criteria

B.E. Admission

Through MHT-CET / JEE Main via CAP rounds conducted by State CET Cell, Maharashtra.

M.E. Admission

Through GATE score. Direct admission available for GATE-qualified candidates.

Lateral Entry

Available for Diploma holders. 10% seats reserved through separate CAP rounds.
